# Miniature Floodstorm Rescue — Video Prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic macro video of a miniature village built on a wooden tabletop during a devastating rainstorm.

Muddy brown floodwater violently rushes through the tiny streets, flooding miniature houses and surrounding small trees. Tiny stylized human villagers panic, running through the water, rescuing their belongings, helping each other, and desperately waving for help from rooftops. Miniature cars are partially submerged, wooden debris floats through the streets, and realistic raindrops create tiny splashes and waves. The camera captures the scene in a dramatic close-up macro perspective, with highly detailed miniature textures, realistic water physics, storm lighting, and cinematic depth of field.

As the flood continues, the tiny villagers desperately struggle through the rising water, pulling their supplies to safety and waving for help. A real human hand enters slowly from the top of the frame holding a large colourful umbrella, moving with calm deliberate purpose. The hand carefully opens the umbrella and lowers it over the flooded village, creating a protective canopy that redirects the falling rain away from the miniature streets. The water gradually settles, and the villagers' homes become safe. The hand slowly withdraws upward, leaving the miniature village protected beneath the umbrella.

Style: photorealistic miniature diorama, cinematic macro cinematography, dramatic storm atmosphere, detailed raindrops and floodwater, realistic reflections, smooth continuous motion, 4K visual quality.

Avoid: cartoonish rendering, low-detail models, distorted hands, inconsistent scale, abrupt camera cuts, text, logos, and watermarks.
