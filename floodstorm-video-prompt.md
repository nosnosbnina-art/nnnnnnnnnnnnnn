# Miniature Floodstorm — Video Prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic macro video of a miniature village built on a wooden tabletop during a devastating rainstorm.

Muddy brown floodwater violently rushes through the tiny streets, flooding miniature houses and surrounding small trees. Tiny stylized human villagers panic, running through the water, rescuing their belongings, helping each other, and desperately waving for help from rooftops.

Miniature cars are partially submerged, wooden debris floats through the streets, and realistic raindrops create tiny splashes and waves.

The camera slowly moves forward at street level, capturing the villagers' desperate struggle.

Photorealistic miniature details, realistic water physics, dramatic cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, 4K quality, vertical 9:16.

No text, no subtitles, no logos.
